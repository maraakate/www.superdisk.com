Question: What are the minimum system configuration requirements for the installation and operation of a SuperDisk Drive of my laptop?

Answer: The Imation SuperDisk Drive is a state-of-the-art PCMCIA interfaced design intended for use as either the primary disk drive or auxiliary disk drive for notebook, subnotebook, or other computers with a PC Card slot.

The minimum configuration requirements are:

  • PCMCIA Release 2 (or greater) Type II or III card slot
  • Power: 5 VDC + or - 5% @ 50 mA if drive is powered by AC adapter or @ 1000 mA peak if drive is powered from system.
  • Software: MS-DOS version 5.0 Windows 95 or Windows 98, and Card Services release 2 or greater of Socket Services release 1.01 or greater. Card and Socket Services are included with Windows 95 and 98.
  • Hardware: PC compatible 486 or greater with 8 MB RAM and 5 MB hard disk space.

Question: My SuperDisk PCMCIA Drive kit came supplied with an AC adapter and PS/2 keyboard adapter, but I forgot to pack them for my trip. Can I substitute another DC power source?

Answer: No. The AC adapter provides a regulated 5 volt DC power source which can be used to power the drive. Use of any DC power source other than those supplied with the SuperDisk Drive may result in damage to the SuperDisk Drive and will consequently void the warranty.

Question: Can I power the SuperDisk Drive from my laptop battery?

Answer: Yes; the SuperDisk PCMCIA Drive accepts power from either the host computer system via the PC Card,PS/2 keyboard adaptor, or from the AC adapter. While most computer's today can normally supply power to the SuperDisk Drive, some computer system's PC Card slots do not supply enough electrical power to properly power the SuperDisk Drive. We recommend you use the AC adapter included in your SuperDisk Drive kit whenever possible to extend your system's battery life.Note: To make sure your system's Card slot can supply adequate power to the SuperDisk Drive, use the AC adapter the first time you power up the drive and then switch to battery power by removing the adapter's DC cable from the SuperDisk Drive.

Question: I've heard my laptop may support "hot swapping" of PC Cards.What does this mean?

Answer: Hot swapping is a useful feature when you need to insert a PCMCIA memory card, modem card, etc., and then replace it with the PCMCIA Interface Card to retrieve or store files to the disk.

Note:

  • Always remove any diskettes from the SuperDisk Drive prior to hot swapping. Never hot swap while the drive is in use, as indicated by the drive's activity light.
  • Prior to removing the PCMCIA Interface Card, always double click on the PCMCIA Card icon on the lower right of the Windows 95 or 98 taskbar. Click on STOP and OK, then remove the PCMCIA Interface Card.

Question: Do I need to format my SuperDisk Diskettes?

Answer: No; SuperDisk Diskettes are preformatted for your convenience at the factory and do not require formatting. If you wish to reformat a SuperDisk Diskette to quickly erase all files and folders, you may use the your system's built in format routine.

Question: Why doesn't my SuperDisk Drive run, even though messages on boot up are normal?

Answer:

  • If you are using the optional AC adapter, check to make sure the DC power cable is properly seated into the SuperDisk Drive power jack and the AC adapter is plugged into an appropriate live AC outlet.
  • If you are powering the drive directly from the host system, make sure the PC Card slot can adequately power the SuperDisk Drive as described in the hardware installation section of your User's Guide.
Note: We recommend using the AC adapter or PS/2 keyboard adapter when initially booting up the SuperDisk Drive for best results. Then you can switch to battery power after boot up if your computer can deliver adequate power to the drive.
