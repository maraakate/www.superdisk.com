Question: Does the Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive fit inside my current computer?

Answer: No; Imation's SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive is an external drive developed for user friendly plug-n-play operation. It connects to the parallel port (printer port) of your PC and can be used with virtually every PC that has a 386 MHz processor or faster, and is running Windows™ 3.x, Windows™ 9X or Windows™ NT 4.0 and higher.

Most IBM-compatible PC computers can also be upgraded with an internal LS-120 drive. Depending on your system's BIOS support the SuperDisk Internal IDE Drive may even be installed to boot from your hard drive. There are now a variety of products on the market so you have choices about how to upgrade your machine. You can install an internal upgrade drive yourself if you are a confident computer hobbyist, or you can ask a retail store to install it for a modest charge.

Question: After I installed my SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive, I was unable to access an application that is protected by a Software Guard (Key). How do I fix this problem?

Answer: The Software Guard must be connected directly to the computer printer port first. Then add any other peripherals, such as the SuperDisk Drive, after the Software Guard.

Question: What is the SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive?

Answer: SuperDisk™ is Imation's trademark name for its LS-120 technology products ­ SuperDisk™ Diskettes and SuperDisk™ LS-120 Drive. The SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive is a user friendly plug-n-play external drive that interfaces with IBM-Compatible PCs to read and write both 120MB SuperDisk Diskettes and all standard 3.5" high-density and double-density diskettes.

SuperDisk Diskettes provide 120 megabytes (MB) of data storage capacity on a single 3.5" diskette ­ that's more than 83 times the capacity of a regular 1.44MB diskette. SuperDisk Diskettes are read/written on a special drive, the SuperDisk Drive or another LS-120 drive.

Imation SuperDisk technology is the time saving answer for transporting and/or storing large amounts of data without cluttering up your hard drive. It is the perfect device for use with large graphics files, project management, complete project drawer, internet downloads, digital photo libraries, large business presentations, video and audio files, and much much more.

Question: How fast is the SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive?

Answer: SuperDisk drives reads twenty three times faster than conventional 1.44MB diskette drives. The Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive (PPD) has a maximum transfer rate of 750 KB/s with SuperDisk Diskettes. An external SuperDisk PPD drive is faster than an external parallel port ZIP drive.

Note: An internal SuperDisk drive reads twenty seven times faster than conventional 1.44 diskette drives.

Question: What kind of interface is used to connect the SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive to my PC?

Answer: The drive interface is IDE/ATAPI, which may be available at the CD-ROM drive port, the hard drive port, or via an expansion card. The drive uses the same power as a floppy drive, so the current cable can be used (or a spare), but the key step is changing over the data cable to the IDE channel connection.

In many newer computers there are four IDE connections (two primary and two secondary). The first primary connection is usually connected to the hard disk drive and the second primary channel is often used for a CD-ROM drive.

The SuperDisk Drive can be connected as a primary or secondary device and it is important to follow the installation instructions that come with the drive. The simplest connection may be to connect the LS-120 drive behind the CD-ROM drive as a secondary device.
