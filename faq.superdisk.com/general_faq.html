Question: I’m having problems formatting my 1.44MB diskette.

Answer: This problem is specific to the NT 4.0 system configuration with Service Pack 3 installed. There are several solutions. Use one of the three solutions offered below that best meets your needs:

  • Follow the procedure outlined in Microsoft's Knowledge Base article Q170572. (This resolution uses the ATAPI.SYS driver from SP2.)
  • Install Microsoft Post SP3 hot fixes; ATA-FIXI.EXE and IDE-FIXI.EXE
  • Upgrade to Service Pack 4 or 5.
NOTE: Visit ftp://ftp.microsoft.com/bussys/winnt/winnt-public/fixes/usa/nt40/hotfixes-postSP3/ for Microsoft NT4 Post SP3 Hotfixes.

Question: Will the standard diskette media hold 120 MB when used with the SuperDisk Drive?

Answer: No. The SuperDisk Drive only supports the 120 MB capacity when used with the SuperDisk (LS-120). The SuperDisk Drive reads and writes to both 1.44 MB and 720 KB disks faster than an ordinary disk drive.

Question: How does the SuperDisk Diskette obtain such high capacity when it looks just like a regular disk?

Answer: The SuperDisk technology achieves its exceptional capabilities based on two basic principles:

  1. Each disk has embedded in the magnetic surface precision laser etched servo tracks. These tracks provide the basis for the high density that contributes to the high capacity. The SuperDisk has 1,736 tracks per side compared to only 80 for a 1.44 MB disk.
  2. The media on a SuperDisk is made from a high performance Metal Particle (MP) magnetic coating. This coating enables higher density recording, and its high output permits the use of the narrower data tracks described above.

Question: Can I format a 1.44 MB disk in my SuperDisk Drive?

Answer: Yes; format your 1.44 MB disk using the current SuperDisk driver version 1.25. If you do not have this driver version, please download the current driver from our web site.

Question: What is SuperDisk technology?

Answer: SuperDisk™ is Imation's trademark name for its high capacity diskette technology products ­ SuperDisk Diskettes and SuperDisk (LS-120) Drives. SuperDisk Diskettes provide 120 megabytes (MB) of data storage capacity on a single 3.5" diskette ­ that's more than 83 times the capacity of a regular 1.44MB diskette.

SuperDisk drives are like 2 drives in one -- they read and write both 120MB SuperDisk diskettes and all standard 3.5" high-density and IBM formatted double-density diskettes. You do need a SuperDisk drive to use the 120MB SuperDisk diskettes.

The SuperDisk Drives can be implemented into your computer system in several options. You can get an internal SuperDisk drive for your notebook or desktop PC from many OEM computer manufacturers. You can add an internal SuperDisk drive to your existing notebook or desktop PC systems, add an external parallel port drive or an external USB drive to your PC, an external USB drive for the MAC or an external PCMCIA drive for your notebook. See the product section on the SuperDisk website.

SuperDisk drives make it easy to store large graphics files, project management, complete project drawer, internet downloads, digital photo libraries, large business presentations, video and audio files, and much much more. It is the perfect device to store large amounts of data without cluttering up your hard drive yet keep your files manageable.

Question: Is a SuperDisk Diskette really a diskette?

Answer: Yes! SuperDisk Diskettes are the same size and shape and work the same way as the 3.5" diskettes with which you are so familiar. SuperDisk Diskettes do require a SuperDisk LS-120 drive or another LS-120 drive. These drives are backwards compatible which means they will read/write the standard 3.5" floppies as well as SuperDisk Diskettes!

Question: Is there any special way I should handle and store my SuperDisk Diskettes?

Answer: Obviously, you'll want to handle your new SuperDisk Diskettes with the same care you use when handling other diskettes; after all, you've probably got some pretty valuable data on them. But they're as rugged as you'd expect regular diskettes to be.

We do recommend that you use the jewel cases provided with the SuperDisk Diskettes for storage and transportation of the diskettes. This will provide additional protection from possible contamination from dust and foreign objects and from getting banged around in your briefcase, backpack or desk drawer.

Question: Can I use my SuperDisk Diskette in my current 1.44 megabyte (MB) drive?

Answer: No; you must have an Imation SuperDisk Drive or another LS-120 drive in order to use your SuperDisk Diskettes. Because LS-120 technology is backwards compatible, however, you can use your standard 1.44MB diskettes in a SuperDisk Drive.

Question: Are there actually 120 megabytes (MB) of usable space on a SuperDisk Diskette?

Answer: Yes; SuperDisk Diskettes are factory-formatted and give you 120MB of storage space. That's equal to the space of 83 regular 1.44MB diskettes. Imagine being able to store the contents of a stack of typed pages 15 feet high on a single disk!

In addition, SuperDisk Diskettes work over ten times faster than regular diskettes (and with Imation's Performance Accelerator Utility, your SuperDisk Drive and Diskettes will appear to run about as fast as your hard drive).

Question: Can I use Windows™ Disk Copy command with the SuperDisk Drive to copy SuperDisk Diskettes?

Answer: No; although Microsoft™ Backup will work with the SuperDisk Drive, the software does not support diskettes larger than 32 megabytes. Microsoft™ has expressed an intention to address the MS Backup™ issue in their next release of the utility.In the meantime, Imation has a software toolkit for the LS-120 SuperDisk Drive. The SuperDisk Tools Kit is available from our internet site at http://store.imation.com and includes the following utilities:•CA Backup - provides fast, easy data protection•CA InnocuLAN Anti-Virus - protects your files with 100% virus detection•Disk Consolidator - combines multiple 3.5" diskettes on a single SuperDisk Diskette•Copy Disk - duplicates SuperDisk diskettes with drag and drop simplicity.
