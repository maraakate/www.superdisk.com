SuperDisk Diskettes with Secured Encryption Technology help you make sure private files stay that way. These powerful diskettes use the 16-pass block "Blowfish" encryption algorithm, yet they're a snap to use.

The software installs in minutes. Then choose your password and drag and drop files into the icon on your desktop. Encryption is automatic. Only users with the right password can open encrypted files, even if diskettes are lost or stolen.

Practical Applications

  • Safeguard financial data
  • Protect proprietary corporate assets
  • Conceal sensitive information
  • Encrypt files at work, at home or on the go

Double Duty
SuperDisk secured diskettes also work like regular SuperDisk diskettes, because they store both encrypted and unencrypted files. Encrypted files remain hidden, while unencrypted files appear on your SuperDisk drive as normal.

Important Advantages

  • Simple: No extensive technical experience or time-consuming procedures required.
  • Universal: Encrypt all types of files, including databases, spreadsheets, graphics and text.
  • Secure: The Blowfish algorithm scrambles data 16 times over. Remember, a lost password renders encrypted files unrecoverable.
