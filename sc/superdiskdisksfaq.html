Question: Will the standard diskette media hold 120 MB when used with the SuperDisk Drive?

Answer: No. The SuperDisk Drive only supports the 120 MB capacity when used with the SuperDisk (LS-120). The SuperDisk Drive reads and writes to both 1.44 MB and 720 KB disks faster than an ordinary disk drive.



Question: How does the SuperDisk obtain such high capacity when it looks just like a regular disk?

Answer: The SuperDisk has several features that enable its exceptional capabilities:

- Each disk has embedded in the magnetic surface precision laser etched servo tracks. These tracks provide the basis for the high density that contributes to the high capacity. The SuperDisk has 1,736 tracks per side compared to only 80 for a 1.44 MB disk.

- The media on a SuperDisk is made from a high performance Metal Particle (MP) magnetic coating. This coating enables higher density recording, and its high output permits the use of the narrower data tracks described above.



Question: Can I format a 1.44 MB disk in my SuperDisk Drive?

Answer: Yes. Format your 1.44 MB disk using the current SuperDisk driver version 1.25. If you do not have this driver version, please download the current driver from our web site.



Question: What is SuperDisk?

Answer: SuperDisk is Imation's trademark name for its LS-120 technology products SuperDisk™ diskettes and SuperDisk™ LS-120 Drive.

SuperDisk™ diskettes provide 120 megabytes (MB) of data storage capacity on a single 3.5" diskette that's more than 83 times the capacity of a regular 1.44MB diskette. SuperDisk diskettes are read/written on a special drive, the SuperDisk drive or another LS-120 drive.

The SuperDisk™ Drive is an external parallel port drive that reads and writes both 120MB SuperDisk diskettes and all standard 3.5" high-density and double-density diskettes.

SuperDisk is the perfect technology to use for large graphics files, project management, complete project drawer, internet downloads, digital photo libraries, large business presentations, video and audio files, and much much more. It is the perfect device to store large amounts of data without cluttering up your hard drive.



Question: Is a SuperDisk diskette really a diskette?

Answer: Yes! SuperDisk diskettes are the same size and shape and work the same way as the 3.5" diskettes with which you are so familiar. SuperDisk diskettes do require a SuperDisk LS-120 drive or another LS-120 drive.



Question: Is there any special way I should handle and store my SuperDisk diskettes?

Answer: Obviously, you'll want to handle your new SuperDisk diskettes with the same care you use when handling other diskettes; after all, you've probably got some pretty valuable data on them. But they're as rugged as you'd expect regular diskettes to be. We do recommend that you use the jewel cases provided with the SuperDisk diskettes for storage and transportation of the diskettes. This will provide additional protection from possible contamination from dust and foreign objects and from getting banged around in your briefcase, backpack or desk drawer.



Question: Can I use my SuperDisk diskette in my current 1.44 megabyte (MB) drive?

Answer: No. You must have an Imation SuperDisk drive or another LS-120 drive in order to use your SuperDisk diskettes. However, you can use your standard 1.44MB diskettes in a SuperDisk LS-120 drive.



Question: Are there actually 120 megabytes (MB) of usable space on a SuperDisk diskette?

Answer: Yes. SuperDisk diskettes are factory-formatted and give you 120MB of storage space. That's equal to the space of 83 regular 1.44MB diskettes. Imagine being able to store the contents of a stack of typed pages 15 feet high on a single disk! In addition, SuperDisk diskettes work up to 5 times faster than regular diskettes (and with Imation's Performance Accelerator Utility, your SuperDisk drive and diskettes will appear to run about as fast as your hard drive).



Question: Will Imation's SuperDisk diskettes work in any LS-120 drive?

Answer: Absolutely. LS-120 drives are manufactured by many companies today, and it is expected that additional companies will be making various versions of the drive as well. SuperDisk diskettes will work in all SuperDisk LS-120 drives.
