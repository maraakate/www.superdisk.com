Question: Can I boot from my SuperDisk LS-120 drive?

Answer: The LS-120 is a bootable drive if your computer is LS-120 ready and has the correct BIOS. You can then boot from an LS-120 upgrade drive, which will be designated as your a: drive. Many newer computers come LS-120 ready and can boot from a built-in LS-120 drive. To find out if your computer is LS-120 ready, contact your computer manufacturer. If your computer is not LS-120 ready, you have three options: 1) If you have flash BIOS, flash-upgrade the BIOS on your existing motherboard; or 2) install the FloppyMAX add-in card from Promise Technology (see below); or 3) install an LS-120-ready motherboard with required BIOS. Otherwise, you can add the LS-120 drive as a non-bootable drive in a free drive bay.

Question: Does the Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive fit inside my current computer?

Answer: No. Imation's SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive is an external drive which can be connected to virtually every PC that has a 486 MHz processor or faster, and is running Windows 3.x, Windows 95 or Windows NT 4.0 and higher. It connects to the parallel port (printer port) of your PC.



Question: Can I use Windows Disk Copy command with the SuperDisk Drive to copy SuperDisks?

Answer: No. The Disk Copy command used in DOS, Windows 3.1, and Windows 95 does not support disks larger than 32 megabytes. A new 'disk copy extension' will be available soon from Imation that will enable the copying of the 120 megabyte SuperDisks. Please check back for updates.



Question: Can I use Microsoft Backup with the SuperDisk Drive?

Answer: Yes and no. MS Backup does work with the drive, but does not support spanning of disks. If the end of the media is detected, all data on the disk is erased.



Question: After I installed my SuperDisk Drive, I was unable to access an application that is protected by a Software Guard (Key). How do I fix this problem?

Answer: The Software Guard must be connected directly to the computer printer port. Then add any other peripherals, such as the SuperDisk drive, after the Software Guard.



Question: What is SuperDisk?

Answer: SuperDisk is Imation's trademark name for its LS-120 technology products SuperDisk diskettes and SuperDisk LS-120 Drive.

SuperDisk diskettes provide 120 megabytes (MB) of data storage capacity on a single 3.5" diskette that's more than 83 times the capacity of a regular 1.44MB diskette. SuperDisk diskettes are read/written on a special drive, the SuperDisk drive or another LS-120 drive.

The SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive reads and writes both 120MB SuperDisk diskettes and all standard 3.5" high-density and double-density diskettes.

The current SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive is an external drive. However, internal SuperDisk drives are available and can be installed on most PCs.

SuperDisk is the perfect technology to use for large graphics files, project management, complete project drawer, internet downloads, digital photo libraries, large business presentations, video and audio files, and much much more. It is the perfect device to store large amounts of data without cluttering up your hard drive.



Question: Will I still be able to use my existing 1.44 megabyte (MB) and 720 kilobyte (K) diskettes in the new SuperDisk drive?

Answer: Yes! One of the truly great advantages of SuperDisk LS-120 technology is the drive's ability to read, write and format (Version 1.25 or higher) with your existing 1.44MB and 720K diskettes. In other words, the data you've already saved is still usable with the newest LS-120 technology. In addition, it's new technology but you already know how to use it! Note: Format your diskettes using Version 1.25 or higher. If you do not have the latest version of our drivers, download the latest version from our web site.



Question: How fast is the SuperDisk™ parallel port drive?

Answer: LS-120 drives are up to five times faster than conventional 1.44MB diskette drives. The Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive has a maximum transfer rate of 290 KB/s (17MB/min) with SuperDisk diskettes. This speed is comparable to that of the parallel port ZIP drive. However, the free Performance Accelerator software included with Imation SuperDisk drives allows you to perform other tasks in the foreground while data transfer takes place in the background.

Question: What kind of interface does the SuperDisk Drive use?

Answer: The drive interface is IDE/ATAPI, which may be available at the CD-ROM drive port, the hard drive port, or via an expansion card. The drive uses the same power as a floppy drive, so the current cable can be used (or a spare), but the key step is changing over the data cable to the IDE channel connection. In many newer computers there are four IDE connections (two primary and two secondary). The first primary connection is usually connected to the hard disk drive and the second primary channel is often used for a CD-ROM drive. The SuperDisk drive can be connected as a primary or secondary device and it is important to follow the installation instructions that come with the drive. The simplest connection may be to connect the LS-120 drive behind the CD-ROM drive as a secondary device. More information on the SuperDisk is available on the SuperDisk page of our site.



Question: Can I upgrade my existing desktop PC with an LS-120 or SuperDisk drive?

Answer: Yes! Imation's SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive requires no internal installation at all (it's as easy to hook up as a printer), and gives you the added benefit of portability between multiple computers, so you can use it at work and take it home to transport your files and projects without hauling around your entire computer. Also, most IBM-compatible PC computers can be upgraded with internal SuperDisk drives. There are now a variety of products on the market so you have choices about how to upgrade your machine. You can install an internal upgrade drive yourself if you are a confident computer hobbyist, or you can ask a retail store to install it for a modest charge.

See www.superdisk.com for the latest information on how to add a SuperDisk to your computer system.
