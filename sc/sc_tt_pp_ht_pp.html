    Check your printer port settings for compatibility with SuperDisk™

  1. Right-click My Computer and click Properties>Device Manager tab>Ports (COM & LPT).
  2. Double-click Printer Port (LPT1) and click Resources tab. Check for conflicts at the bottom of the window.
  3. Resolve any conflicts.
  4. On the Resources tab, make sure the input/output address is set to either 0378 or 0278 (don't use 03BC).
  5. Make sure IRQ (interrupt request) 5 or 7 is assigned. Note: IRQ 5 is usually reserved for the sound card.
  6. Check printer port settings in your system BIOS. Set the port mode to EPP. Use ECP, PS2, or bi-directional modes if your system doesn't support EPP.
