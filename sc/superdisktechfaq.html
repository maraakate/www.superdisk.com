Question: What is SuperDisk?

Answer: SuperDisk is Imation's trademark name for its LS-120 technology products

SuperDisk diskettes and SuperDisk LS-120 Drive.

SuperDisk diskettes provide 120 megabytes (MB) of data storagecapacity on a single 3.5" diskette that's more than 83 times the capacity of aregular 1.44MB diskette. SuperDisk diskettes are read/written on a specialdrive, the SuperDisk drive or another LS-120 drive.

The SuperDisk Drive is an external parallel port drive that reads and writesboth 120MB SuperDisk diskettes and all standard 3.5" high-density anddouble-density diskettes.

SuperDisk is the perfect technology to use for large graphics files, projectmanagement, complete project drawer, internet downloads, digital photolibraries, large business presentations, video and audio files, and much much more. It is the perfect device to store large amounts of data without cluttering up your hard drive.



Question: What is LS-120 technology?

Answer: LS-120 technology (the LS stands for laser servo) uses a unique manufacturing process to increase the data track density on a 3.5" diskette from 135 tracks per inch (tpi) to 2490 tpi. Imagine moving the grooves in an old-fashioned record album much closer together so you could record a lot more music on the same album. This is similar to what the LS-120 technology does. A laser servo motor positions the LS-120 drive's read/write head precisely over the correct track on the diskette for accurate data reading/writing. That's why so many people in the computer industry believe the technology will replace the 1.44 megabyte (MB) diskette in new computers.

Note: SuperDisk LS-120 diskettes require a special LS-120 drive; they cannot be read or written in 1.44MB drives.

Key features of the LS-120 technology include the following:

- Enables users to store 120MB of data on a single, 3.5" diskette.

- Represents a capacity increase of more than 83 times over the standard 3.5" diskette

- Is completely backward compatible with the installed base of over 10 billion 3.5" diskettes. It reads and writes 1.44MB and 720K DOS-formatted diskettes as well as the new 120MB format. The technology also includes embedded support under the Windows 95 (OSR2) and Windows NT 4.0 operating systems.

- Delivers up to five times the performance of standard 1.44MB diskette technology, significantly increasing user productivity.

Users can: - Transport large files on a single diskette Download and save internet and network information

- Store large or multiple work-in-progress files on a single diskette

- Save color, sound and motion files without compression

- Store and retrieve fax images, E-mail and phone messages - Save hard disk space

- Store multiple program files for a single project on one diskette

- While the SuperDisk LS-120 diskettes and drives look much the same as the standard 1.44MB diskettes and drives, the technical differences are substantial. Below is a comparison of key specifications:

LS-120 technology was developed by Imation Corporation, Compaq Computer Corp., Matsushita-Kotobuki Industries, Ltd. (MKE), and O.R. Technology. More information on SuperDisk is available on the SuperDisk page of our site.



Question: What's the difference between SuperDisk and LS-120?

Answer: SuperDisk is a licensed trademark owned by Imation Corp. for use on Imation LS-120 drives and diskettes; Imation also licenses the use of the SuperDisk trademark to approved manufacturers of SuperDisk-related products - both drives and diskettes.



Question: Will the LS-120 technology work with my operating system?

Answer: LS-120 technology will work with Windows 3.1x, Windows 95 and Windows NT. We are currently working to expand the support (OS/2 and UNIX), but we cannot provide additional details at this time. We will post information on additional operating systems as it becomes available.

More information on LS-120 technology is available on the SuperDisk page of our site.
