Imation Launches Faster, Sleeker SuperDisk Drives at PC Expo

NEW YORK --- (June 21, 1999) Imation Corp. (NYSE:IMN) today announced four new and improved SuperDisk drives offering faster performance, lightweight design, and simple installation including a new SuperDisk USB Drive for PC and Macintosh.

While previous-generation SuperDisk drives from Imation read data about 10 times faster than a floppy drive, the new and improved SuperDisk drives read much faster -- up to 27 times faster -- than a floppy.

The new drives are capable of handling multimedia applications such as video and audio recording while retaining the reliability and compatibility advantages that have made SuperDisk technology the leading replacement for the floppy.

Like all SuperDisk drives, the new products are fully read and write compatible with floppies and 120 MB SuperDisk diskettes.

"Storage demands keep accelerating, so its no surprise that SuperDisk continues to gain momentum," said Steve Ladwig, president, Imation Data Storage and Information Management. "Imations family of SuperDisk drives presents our customers with a better array of storage options than ever before. With SuperDisk, theres virtually no limit to the amount of images, photos, video and audio files users can share and store easily, efficiently and inexpensively with any kind of computer."

Imation SuperDisk USB Drive for PC and Macintosh

An entirely new product from Imation, the Imation SuperDisk USB Drive for PC and Macintosh offers PC and Macintosh USB compatibility in a hot-swappable, portable drive. It reads data up to 22 times faster than a floppy drive and is ideal for use with any computer with a USB port, especially those desktop and notebook PCs that lack an onboard floppy drive. It will be available in August at $169.99 (MSRP actual pricing may vary).

Imation SuperDisk USB Drive for Macintosh

One of the most popular storage products for iMac and Power Macintosh G3 computers from Apple, the new and improved Imation SuperDisk USB Drive for Macintosh now reads data up to 22 times faster than a floppy drive. It boasts an "all-ice" design to complement all of the latest Macintosh colors. It offers easy plug-and-play setup in seconds for any Mac with a USB interface and is hot-swappable. The new, faster drive is now available at $169.99 (MSRP actual pricing may vary).

Imation SuperDisk Internal IDE Drive

Offering an easy-to-install upgrade for almost any desktop PC, the new and improved Imation SuperDisk Internal Drive reads data up to 27 times faster than a floppy drive. It installs on most systems as the bootable a: drive giving the user high capacity storage, excellent performance and dual compatibility in a single drive bay. It is available now at $99.99 (MSRP actual pricing may vary). Imation is offering a $20 rebate on the product, if purchased at retail, until Dec. 31, 1999.

Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive

Sporting a sleek, more portable design, the redesigned and improved Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive now reads data up to 23 times faster than a floppy drive. Lighter and more portable than its predecessor, it can be easily attached to a computers printer port and is the easy way to add storage capacity to any PC. It is available now at $149.99 (MSRP actual pricing may vary). Imation is offering a $20 rebate on the product, if purchased at retail, until Dec. 31, 1999.

Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive

Sporting a sleek, more portable design, the redesigned and improved Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive now reads data up to 23 times faster than a floppy drive. Lighter and more portable than its predecessor, it can be easily attached to a computers printer port and is the easy way to add storage capacity to any PC. All of the new and improved Imation SuperDisk drives include the necessary software drivers, power adapters, cabling and instructions for simple installation.

Imations line of SuperDisk products also include the 120 MB Imation SuperDisk Diskette and the Imation SuperDisk Diskette with Secured Encryption Technology. SuperDisk drives also are offered as standard or optional internal equipment in new desktop and notebook computers from more than 100 leading PC suppliers. Additionally, SCSI and PCMCIA SuperDisk drives are available from leading suppliers. More information about SuperDisk technology is available at http://www.superdisk.com.
