Imation Ships One-Millionth SuperDisk USB Drive For Macintosh

SuperDisk is Number-One Selling Storage Device Among Apple iMac Owners

OAKDALE, Minn. (January 5, 2000) Imation Corp. (NYSE:IMN) today announced shipment of the one-millionth SuperDisk USB Drive for Macintosh, the high-capacity, floppy-compatible storage device designed especially for Apples new family of iMacs.

With 120MB capacity and compatibility with floppies, the SuperDisk drive is an ideal complement to iMac, iMac DV, and iMac DV Special Edition. Users can choose to use either 1.44MB floppies for backing up, storing and sharing smaller files, or 120MB SuperDisk diskettes for larger files.

Almost half of iMac owners have chosen SuperDisk for high-capacity and floppy storage, making SuperDisk the most popular storage device for iMac, said Debra Rectenwald, SuperDisk Business Director for Imation. The incredible popularity of iMac and SuperDisk is validation of outstanding technology and great design.

The wildly popular iMac presents great opportunities for developers on the Mac, said Clent Richardson, Apples Vice President of Worldwide Developer Relations. With SuperDisk, Imation provides iMac owners with an excellent high-capacity storage device. Imation will feature a special "Thanks a Million, Mac" celebration in its booth at Macworld Expo, January 5-8, in San Francisco. Visitors to Imation (Booth #425) will receive a coupon good for a $50 rebate on the purchase of a SuperDisk USB drive for Macintosh during Macworld Expo.

The Imation SuperDisk USB Drive is available for $149.99 (MSRP - actual pricing may vary) on www.apple.com and wherever Apple products are sold. Imation also is currently offering a $10 rebate with the purchase of a five-pack of Mac-formatted Imation SuperDisk Diskettes. See retailer for details and rebate form.

About Imation Corp.

A global technology company, Imation Corp. supplies a variety of products and services worldwide for the information and image management industry, specializing in data storage and information management, color management and imaging solutions.
