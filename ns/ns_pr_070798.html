Imation Announces Pricing, Availability For SuperDisk USB Products

NEW YORK --- (July 7, 1998) -- On the eve of the opening of MacWorld Expo, Imation Corp. (NYSE: IMN) today announced details about new, 120 MB SuperDisk products designed for compatibility with the new Apple iMac Computer and the USB (Universal Serial Bus) interface.

Developed by Imation and Panasonic, and to be demonstrated for the first time at MacWorld (Imation booth #838), the floppy-compatible Imation SuperDisk USB Drive is expected to be available in North America beginning the week of August 16 at a manufacturers suggested retail price of $189 (actual prices may vary). At the same time, Imation will launch the Imation Mac-Formatted SuperDisk Diskette, which initially will be available in a five pack at a manufacturers suggested retail price of $64.99 (actual prices may vary).

"iMac combines the excitement of the Internet with the simplicity of the Macintosh," said Phil Schiller, senior director of worldwide product marketing at Apple Computer Inc. "The industry is lining up to support iMac and it is great to see a leading vendor like Imation quickly bring their popular and leading edge SuperDisk technology out in time for iMac's introduction in August."

The SuperDisk diskette provides 120 MB of high-capacity storage Apple iMac users will need for many of their applications. At the same time, the SuperDisk drive is fully compatible with highly affordable 1.44 MB and 720 KB diskettes - of which there are billions in the world today. No other available technology provides this combination of high capacity and floppy compatibility in a single device.

Imations SuperDisk USB Drive and Mac-Formatted SuperDisk Diskettes will be available via Apples retail, catalog and educational channels, as well as many of Imations distribution channels. Global availability of these products will follow in coming months.

"As the leading replacement for the 1.44 MB diskette drive in new PCs, SuperDisk technology is a natural fit for the capacity and compatibility needs of the Mac user," said Ray Meifert, SuperDisk business director, Imation. "As the introduction of the exciting new Apple iMac nears, were pleased that iMac purchasers will be able to specify a SuperDisk drive to accompany their new computers - right at the time of purchase. With the tremendous excitement building around SuperDisk technology, we think that iMac buyers will find Imations SuperDisk USB Drive the high-capacity storage device best suited to meet their needs."

Along with the new SuperDisk USB products shown at this weeks MacWorld Expo, Imation also is highlighting other SuperDisk options for the Mac user, including internal and external SCSI SuperDisk drives from Winstation Systems. Imation, a co-developer of SuperDisk technology, also markets the Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive and the Imation SuperDisk Diskette. More information about SuperDisk technology is available at http://www.superdisk.com.

Imation Corp. supplies a variety of products and services worldwide for the imaging and information industry. Imation is the worlds leading supplier of branded, removable media for data storage applications. Imation products are supported by a worldwide customer service organization. In 1997, the company generated revenues of $2.2 billion. Imation employs approximately 9,500 people and is based in Oakdale, Minn.
