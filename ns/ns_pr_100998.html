Imation Corp. To Provide SuperDisk Technology to School Districts, Non-Profits Nationwide

OAKDALE, Minn. (October 9, 1998) -- Imation Corp. (NYSE: IMN) today announced that it will donate 5,000 Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drives to school districts and non-profit organizations in communities across the United States. As a component of the companys corporate giving efforts, the donation is consistent with Imations efforts to reinforce the importance of technology in education and to support non-profit organizations by assisting them with their technology needs.

The latest in data storage technology, SuperDisk enables users to save 120 megabytes of information (the equivalent of 46,680 typed pages) on a SuperDisk diskette while also allowing users to continue use of their 1.44 floppy diskettes -- all in the same drive.

"We are pleased to bring our SuperDisk technology to schools and non-profit organizations," said Bill Monahan, Imation chairman and chief executive officer. "We believe that encouraging the use of technology in education plays an important part in preparing students to meet the technology challenges of the twenty-first century. The primary focus of our philanthropic program is education, with a particular interest in identifying ways that technology can enhance the learning process and help non-profits increase their effectiveness through the use of technology."

Imations other corporate giving efforts include the annual Imation Computer Arts Scholarship Program, a unique approach to encourage the use of technology in arts education. Through participation this Imation program, talented high school students who create original works of computer-generated art have the opportunity to receive one of twenty-five $1,000 scholarships for their creativity in combining art and technology.

SuperDisk technology was co-developed by Imation Corp. which sells the Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive and SuperDisk Diskette. As the only available high-capacity diskette technology that also provides compatibility with 1.44 MB and 720 KB diskettes, SuperDisk technology was designed as the next evolution of the 1.44 MB floppy drive.

Imation Corp. supplies a variety of products and services worldwide forthe information and image management industry. Imation is the worldsleading supplier of branded, removable media for data storageapplications. Imation products are supported by worldwide customerservice organization. In 1997, the company generated revenues of $2.2 billion. Imation employs approximately 9,000 people and is based in Oakdale, Minn.
