SuperDisk Photo Scrapbook


Imation deskettes march through Union Station in Washington, D.C. to tell computer users in the nation's capital about SuperDisk, a 120 MB diskette that holds 83 times the data of a 1.44 MB diskette. Plus, SuperDisk is backward compatible. So you can use your existing 1.44 MB diskettes and SuperDisk in the same drive!


SuperDisk and his diskette friends join to protest the impending obsolescence of 1.44 MB diskettes. SuperDisk is the only technology available that allows computer users to use their existing technology with new technology. Save a little with 1.44 MB diskettes of save a lot with SuperDisk!


The DC lunchtime crowd gathered at Union Station to learn about the benefits of SuperDisk. Hundreds of people took part in SuperDisk demonstrations and raffles. Fifteen lucky winners went home with brand new SuperDisk parallel port drives.
