Updating Macs to 120 MB, floppy-compatible SuperDisk drives easy with Imation SuperDisk diskette, Keyspan USB PCI card bundle

OAKDALE, Minn. (January 5, 1998) Hot-pluggable USB SuperDisk drives which have proved extremely popular among iMac users are now accessible to millions of Macintosh users with a new bundle announced today by Imation Corp. (NYSE: IMN) and Keyspan at Macworld Expo San Francisco 99.

Designed to provide a simple, affordable way to update Macs with the Imation USB SuperDisk drive, the bundle includes a Keyspan USB Card that allows users to add two standard Universal Serial Bus (USB) ports to any PCI-based Macintosh running Mac OS 8.1 or above; and three Imation SuperDisk Mac-formatted diskettes. The Keyspan card includes drivers for the Imation USB SuperDisk drive, making it truly as easy as plug-and-play for Mac users to add a SuperDisk drive. The card and diskette bundle is available immediately at a suggested retail price of $79 (Keyspans USB PCI card alone retails for $59; a three-pack of Imation SuperDisk diskettes regularly retails for $49.99).

Once the card is installed, users can simply plug in the Imation SuperDisk USB drive and begin using the Imation SuperDisk 120 MB diskettes immediately for backup, archiving, storage and file sharing. The Imation SuperDisk USB drive can be purchased for a suggested retail price of $149.

According to Ray Meifert, Imation Corps SuperDisk business director, schools, computer labs and other environments that mix new and old Macintosh and iMac systems are looking for an easy way to use 120 MB, floppy-compatible SuperDisk drives and diskettes with both computers.

"Apple iMac users who have used the Imation SuperDisk USB drive as their floppy diskette and high-capacity removable storage solution have seen first hand the compatibility and familiarity of SuperDisk drives and diskettes," Meifert said. "We are excited to work with Keyspan to make it easy for standard Mac users to take advantage of the SuperDisk platform as well."

"USB is quickly becoming the interface of choice for many new Macs and PCs, and the availability of USB devices is growing rapidly," said Mike Ridenhour, president, Keyspan. "Our card adds USB to any PCI-based Macmaking USB solutions like SuperDisk drives available to millions of Macintosh owners." SuperDisk technology, designed as the next evolution of the 1.44 MB floppy drive, is a 120 MB diskette technology that works with 120 MB SuperDisk Diskettes as well as the billions of 1.44 MB and 720 KB diskettes that are sold every year.

SuperDisk drives are available to meet a wide range of user needs for desktop and notebook storage. Imation, a developer of SuperDisk technology, also markets the Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive, the Imation SuperDisk USB drive, the Imation SuperDisk Diskette and Imation SuperDisk Diskette with Secured Encryption Technology. SuperDisk drives also are offered as standard or optional internalequipment in new desktop and notebook computers from more than 70 leading suppliers, including Apples PowerBook G3 Series. SCSI, PCMCIA, and internal IDE SuperDisk drives are available from various suppliers. More information about SuperDisk technology is available at http://www.superdisk.com.

Keyspan's products include the SX Serial Card, a low cost serial expansion card and the SMART SERIAL 6, a high-speed serial communications, which supports T1 data rates.
