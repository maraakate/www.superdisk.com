Micron Electronics Offers SuperDisk Drives With Its Corporate and Consumer Desktop PCs

Micron Electronics now is offering SuperDisk 120 MB drives in Micron(TM) Millennia and Micron ClientPro desktop PCs, officials of Micron and Imation Corp. announced today.

Purchasers of Micron's Millennia and Client Pro desktop PCs now have the option of ordering an internal, bootable SuperDisk drive, in lieu of a 1.44 MB floppy drive, for an additional $79.

``As file sizes grow and our customers' storage needs continue to intensify, SuperDisk technology presents a floppy-compatible, user-friendly solution,'' said Jeff Moeser, Micron's vice president of desktop products. ``With a single, internal SuperDisk drive, Micron's customers now can store and exchange high-capacity files while retaining access to their library of floppies without adding a peripheral device. We're pleased to bring this innovative option to Micron's customers.''

Imation is a developer of floppy-compatible SuperDisk technology, which is designed to replace the 1.44 MB floppy drive. The SuperDisk drive is the only available high-capacity diskette technology that works with SuperDisk 120 MB diskettes as well as 1.44 MB and 720 KB diskettes.

``With today's news, Micron extends the benefits of SuperDisk technology to its substantial base of corporate and government PC customers as well as consumer users,'' said Debra Rectenwald, SuperDisk business director, Imation. ``As the array of SuperDisk products and options continues to grow, Micron's customers will find ever-increasing value in SuperDisk technology.''

Imation's current line of SuperDisk products includes the Imation SuperDisk Diskette, the Imation SuperDisk Diskette with Secured Encryption Technology, and the Imation SuperDisk Parallel Port Drive. SuperDisk drives also are offered as standard or optional internal equipment in new desktop and notebook computers from more than 100 leading PC suppliers. Additionally, SCSI, PCMCIA, and internal IDE SuperDisk drives are available from various leading suppliers. More information about SuperDisk technology is available at http://www.superdisk.com.
