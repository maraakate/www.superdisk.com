EPISODE 1: CAPACITY CATASTROPHE!

11:49 p.m. You can smell the pot of coffee that's been sitting on the burner since noon.

It's been a long day for the team in the main workroom. Hidden among the stacks of manila folders, graffitied flip-charts and printouts taped up everywhere, they've been here since 6:00 a.m. But they can't leave until the project's finished. And right now there seems to be a problem...

"Why won't it fit on the diskette? It's just a little electronic presentation!" says the one in the khaki pants.

"Little yes, but it's still three megs," says the one running the mouse. "It's not going to fit on a floppy. Can't we put it on one of these 100MB diskettes?"

"No way! I have to take it with me on the laptop. Do we have a 100MB drive in the laptop?"

"No. We're toast."

From out of nowhere, a brilliant flash of light. A strange, powerful figure emerges-eyes shining brightly and blue plastic gleaming.

"Looking for higher capacity? Sounds like a job for... well, me!"

It's the Imation SuperDisk solution, ready for action. Just as quickly as he appears, he blasts inside the team's computers.

"What's going on in there?" shouts khaki pants. "Are we going to be OK?"

Without warning, the desktop and notebook are transformed, outfitted with two-in-one SuperDisk drives and a stack of 120MB diskettes. Another flash of light, and our hero is back on the scene.

"You're all set! With SuperDisk drives in your desktop and notebook, you can save huge files on the first-and transport them on the second, no problem."

"So we're OK now? We can go home?"

"Absolutely! Not only can you keep the presentation on a 120MB SuperDisk diskette, you can use these high-capacity diskettes to keep your hard drive free of excess data. Which helps it run more efficiently."

The team smiles with the knowledge they'll be home soon. It's another victory for high-capacity SuperDisk diskettes and drives.
