He's the hottest rising star in the data storage world right now, with high-capacity diskettes and a miraculous two-in-one drive. We caught up with him recently at Chez Disque, an out-of-the-way bistro in SuperDisk World, for a little quid pro quo.

I guess the big question on everyone's lips is: where did you come from?
Actually, I was invented-or "developed," as they say-by a collection of global manufacturers to replace 1.44MB technology. It was a group effort by Imation, Compaq and MKE (Panasonic).
I see. So, what's the deal with LS-120?
That's me. I mean, Imation SuperDisk solutions and LS-120 technology are one and the same-but I've had a name change. The "LS" stands for "laser servo," which refers to the method used to fit 120MB on a 3.5-inch diskette. "SuperDisk" is the brand name, and I think it does a better job describing what I do.
You seem to be everywhere these days. What's your schedule like?
Busy! More than 100 manufacturers are putting SuperDisk solutions in their desktops and notebooks. That's a lot of saving... but I love the work.
Care to name names?
Compaq. Dell. IBM. Toshiba. Gateway. Apple. Hewlett Packard. NEC. Micron. Siemens-Nixdorf. Fujitsu. Panasonic. There's more, too, if you want to check out the Product Showroom.
Sounds like you're becoming a standard!
You could say that.
Pretty incredible for a new technology. Is it your super powers that make you so... attractive?
I like to think so. It's not every 3.5-inch diskette that can hold 120MB. That's the equivalent of 83 floppies-but who's counting? I just want to make it easy for people to keep applications and files and what-have-you off their hard drives. And take huge files with them wherever they go on a single diskette.
I understand your drive is pretty unique, too.
Yes, it's two drives in one. It's the only drive out there with proven backwards compatibility. Which means every SuperDisk drive can read SuperDisk diskettes as well as conventional 3.5-inch floppies.
Is it pretty complicated?
No, not at all. In fact, SuperDisk diskettes and drives work just like the floppies everyone's been using for years-with drag-and-drop simplicity.
And you're pretty fast?
Up to 27 times times faster than any floppy drive. There's also SuperDisk Accelerator, which is software that helps every SuperDisk drive run in the background.
What does that mean?
It means you return to what you were doing faster, and the SuperDisk drive finishes up while you're working on other things. Just like you would if you were saving to your hard disk drive.
Speaking of saving... I heard something about your secret encryption powers. Can you give me the details?
Certainly. SuperDisk Diskettes with Secured Encryption Technology work like normal SuperDisk diskettes. But they include high-powered encryption software that can automatically encode files as you save them. And you have to have the right password to open encrypted files.
Perfect for my hottest front-page scoops! Which reminds me... you have a couple secrets I need to ask about, OK?
Absolutely.
What keeps your hair so strikingly vertical... and so orange?
Let's just say it's another of my super powers-I can't give everything away!
