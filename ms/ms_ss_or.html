EPISODE 2:
 TERROR AT 10,000 FEET

Far away from her home, cruising at an altitude of 10,000 feet, a normally calm executive is locked in a battle to the death-with a notebook computer.

Bullets of sweat bead on her forehead as she attempts to extract it from the overhead compartment. The strap from the carrying case is wrapped around the connector cables from her add-on, high-capacity drive.

She pulls mightily, yanking it free. In her cramped coach seat, she attempts to assemble the components. After minutes of untangling cords, plugging in cables and booting everything up, she discovers the extra drive's battery is dead.

The flight attendant asks if she would care for a bag of honey-roasted peanuts.

Before violence erupts, a curiously charming voice booms out.

"Can I be of some assistance?"

It's the SuperDisk solution, standing tall in his bright blue outfit.

"You've got to help me. I have to finish this report before we land. With all the applications involved, I had to use high-capacity diskettes. But this add-on unit is driving me crazy!"

"That's the perfect job for SuperDisk diskettes and drives. Just watch!"

In a flash, our hero blasts off at rocket-speed, shooting directly into the notebook. When the smoke clears, he stands smiling near the forlorn executive, brandishing a pair of SuperDisk diskettes.

"What did you just do?"

"I just installed a SuperDisk drive inside your notebook! SuperDisk drives are the only high-capacity, floppy-compatible drives that fit comfortably in notebook computers. Now, you can transport as much data as you need on handy 120MB diskettes-without the extra cables, cords and hassle of an add-on drive."

He drops the diskettes on the tray table.

"What a fantastic solution! I owe everything to my Imation SuperDisk diskettes and drives!"

In a flash, our hero disappears into the night, in search of more struggling notebook systems to rescue.
